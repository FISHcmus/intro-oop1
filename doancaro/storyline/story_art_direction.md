# Story Art Direction

## Core Style

- Bright `wuxia painting cartoon`, not dark fantasy realism
- Hand-painted 2D look with visible brush rhythm and watercolor paper texture
- Child-friendly shapes, readable silhouettes, soft faces, expressive emotions
- Vietnamese folklore mood with decorative cloud, water, mountain, and tiled-roof motifs

## Palette

- Warm sunrise gold
- Celadon jade
- Sky blue
- Peach and lotus pink accents
- Ivory paper background tone
- Antique gold details

Avoid muddy gray, horror contrast, blood-red dominance, and heavy desaturation.

## Character Rules

- The heroine stays recognizably Vietnamese and youthful
- Hair, clothing, and emotion can change between scenes to match the beat
- Costumes should stay in the same family: scholar-princess, flowing sleeves, teal/jade/gold base
- Faces should be stylized and gentle, never hyper-real or glossy

## Composition Rules

- One clear emotional beat per image
- Keep breathing room for story text overlays
- Favor medium-wide and wide compositions over tight glamour close-ups
- Use flatter painted values instead of cinematic lens realism

## Do Not Do

- Photoreal oil-painting drama
- Grim apocalypse tone
- Western medieval armor or castle language
- Horror creature faces
- Over-detailed skin rendering
- Generic anime gloss with plastic highlights
